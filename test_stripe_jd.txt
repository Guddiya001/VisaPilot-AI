Senior Backend Platform Engineer

Dublin, Ireland (Hybrid)

About Stripe:
Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for the internet. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities.

Responsibilities:

- Maintain transactional consistency, zero transaction loss, and strict MAS/PCI-DSS compliance standards.
- Troubleshoot low-level Linux networking, socket buffer bottlenecks, and concurrency deadlocks.
- Optimize CI/CD pipelines, container orchestration on Kubernetes, and Terraform infrastructure as code.
- Champion engineering excellence, mentor junior/mid-level engineers, and communicate with executive stakeholders.

Requirements:
- 6+ years of backend software engineering experience building mission-critical distributed systems.
- Strong proficiency in Go (Golang), Python, C++, and Rust.

- Experience with event-driven architectures utilizing Kafka and RabbitMQ.
- Strong administration experience with relational and analytical databases (PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, Redis).
- Proven experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, and Linux troubleshooting.
- Experience with telemetry, distributed tracing (OpenTelemetry), and Prometheus monitoring.
- Excellent stakeholder communication, international collaboration, and project ownership.
